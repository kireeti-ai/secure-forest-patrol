# Phase 4.3 Review Protocol

Review original audio locally and preserve the original signal. Do not use filenames, metadata, model predictions, or objective waveform statistics as proof of a class. Record ACCEPTED, RELABEL_REQUIRED, AMBIGUOUS, EXCLUDED, or NEEDS_SECOND_REVIEW. A target requires a clearly audible class and boundaries when applicable; background recordings do not require event boundaries. Low-confidence, mixed, or disputed cases remain non-eligible until adjudicated.

The first batch is source-balanced: 30 background, 20 chainsaw, and 10 gunshot candidates. The full queue remains available. Review decisions are separate from the manifest and must include reviewer ID and an ISO-8601 UTC timestamp.
