# License Resolution

This is a conservative dataset-use audit, not legal advice. C3GD is treated as eligible with attribution based on the cited repository; ESC-50 and RFCx are research-only because their cited terms are non-commercial; FSC22 and Rodopi remain quarantined because authoritative recording-level terms were not verified. A repository license is not assumed to cover every underlying recording.

Sensing the Forest is documented as a potential CC0 source in the Phase 4.1 acquisition plan, but no audio was acquired in Phase 4.2. The candidate-level unresolved IDs are listed in `license_audit.csv`.
