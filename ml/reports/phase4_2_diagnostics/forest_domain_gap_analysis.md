# Forest-Domain Gap Analysis

No new forest audio was acquired in Phase 4.2. Sensing the Forest remains relevant because frozen v1 contains zero `sensing_forest` sources, but the official record exposes an approximately 24.4 GB archive and no justified small file-level subset was available through the investigated path. Downloading the entire archive would not satisfy the targeted-acquisition constraint.

The next step is a provider-approved subset of at least 20 distinct forest recordings covering quiet ambience, wind/microphone noise, birds/insects, rain, and low-level environmental conditions, with source IDs, file hashes, metadata, and license confirmation. These recordings must still undergo human listening review; environmental metadata is not proof of a hard negative.
